Methodology: nominal annual savings are modelled as offset energy (generation × self-consumption, capped at usage) × the escalating tariff, with generation reduced yearly by panel degradation. IRR and NPV are computed on the resulting cash flow starting with the upfront system cost. LCOE divides discounted lifetime cost (system cost + O&M) by discounted lifetime generation. This is a planning estimate, not a quote — actual results depend on installer pricing, roof orientation, shading, and future tariff decisions.
